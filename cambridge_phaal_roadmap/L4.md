# Layer 4: Market & Value Proposition (Dual-Loop OS v31)

Layer 4 addresses vertical market applications where the cost of failure is extremely high, positioning Dual-Loop OS as an indispensable white-box safety and decision guidance system.

---

## 🏛️ High-Stakes Application Verticals

1. **Elderly Care & Wearable Safety**:
   - Zero-privacy-leakage local monitoring for fall detection, biochemical anomalies, and cognitive decline support.

2. **Biochemical & Rare Disease Guardrails**:
   - Deterministic filtering of contra-indicated dietary ingredients and drug-drug interactions (e.g., Wilson's disease copper avoidance, PKU phenylalanine boundaries).

3. **Financial Liquidity & Risk Guardrails**:
   - Hard capital protection boundaries preventing catastrophic financial decisions during market volatility.

4. **Automotive & Industrial Autonomous Control**:
   - Integration with ISO 26262 automotive safety standards to guarantee zero unsafe control states in autonomous agents.
