\begin{abstract}
Joint Embedding Predictive Architectures (JEPAs) provide powerful generative latent world models for multi-objective decision making, yet their deployment in safety-critical systems is hindered by out-of-distribution (OOD) latent drift and the absence of hard safety guarantees. We present \textbf{Dual-Loop OS}, a dual-timescale neuro-symbolic architecture for discrete-time systems under bounded additive disturbances and latent drift ($\|\xi\| \le \xi_{\max}$). The architecture coordinates: (1) a Slow Track running a CRATE Transformer encoder ($E_\theta: \mathbb{R}^4 \to \mathbb{R}^{13}$) structuring latent representations via $\mathrm{MCR}^2$---decoupling a 9D motive manifold $\mathcal{M}_{9\mathrm{D}}$ (theoretically inspired by multi-objective goal taxonomies, e.g., Talevich et al., 2017) from a 4D safety subspace $\mathcal{S}_{4\mathrm{D}}$---generating candidate actions via latent MPPI planning; and (2) a Fast Track deterministically evaluating actions against a 14-hyperplane pre-sintered safety polytope in closed-form ($1.08\,\mu\mathrm{s}$, 233 cycles @ 216 MHz on Cortex-M7 instruction timing model). Inductive mathematical proofs (Theorems 1B and 1C) guarantee discrete-time forward invariance ($B(S_t) \ge \epsilon$) and finite-time margin restoration under active brake override. Across 50,000 steps ($N=50$ episodes) on CartPole-v1 ($E_\theta: \mathbb{R}^4 \to \mathbb{R}^{13}$), Dual-Loop OS achieves $0.0 \pm 0.0\%$ safety violations with $97.8 \pm 0.5\%$ success rate, yielding $6.33\times$ marginal safety leverage over unshielded policies. Non-parametric Fisher's exact test ($p = 3.44 \times 10^{-4}$) and non-overlapping 95\% Clopper-Pearson exact intervals ($[0.00\%, 7.11\%]$ vs $[13.06\%, 38.17\%]$) confirm statistically significant violation elimination.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Latent world models, such as Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture (JEPA), provide expressive latent representations for autonomous decision-making. However, JEPA lacks mathematical safety guarantees, risking out-of-distribution (OOD) dynamic prediction drift in safety-critical applications. 

To resolve this fundamental tension between representation learning and safety, we present \textbf{Dual-Loop OS}, a neuro-symbolic safety architecture that constrains a JEPA-based latent decision system via formal control barrier functions (CBFs).

\section{Related Work}

\subsection{Control Barrier Functions \& Safety Filters}
Control Barrier Functions (CBFs; \citealp{ames2019control,tayal2023exponential}) provide formal set-theoretic guarantees for forward invariance in continuous control-affine systems. Traditional safe control architectures formulate a Quadratic Program (CBF-QP) solved online at each cycle. However, online optimization introduces significant computational latency ($4.8 \sim 15\,\mathrm{ms}$) and risk of infeasibility near boundary intersections. While Explicit MPC \citep{bemporad2002explicit} pre-computes polyhedral regions for microsecond execution, it suffers from exponential critical region explosion ($N_{\mathrm{regions}} > 10^5$) in high dimensions. Dual-Loop OS resolves this limitation by replacing online QP solvers with a pre-sintered $O(1)$ polyhedral feasibility bitmask filter.

\subsection{Safe Exploration in World Models \& Latent Spaces}
In model-based reinforcement learning and World Models (DreamerV2/V3; \citealp{hafner2020mastering,hafner2023mastering}), policies are optimized within compact latent imagination spaces. However, enforcing safety constraints in latent spaces remains challenging. Constrained World Models \citep{as2022constrained} and Latent CBFs \citep{zhao2023latent} incorporate heuristic cost penalties into the Lagrangian objective, which cannot guarantee zero boundary violations under out-of-distribution drift. In non-contrastive Joint Embedding Predictive Architectures (JEPA; \citealp{lecun2022path,bardes2024vjepa}), representation learning lacks explicit geometric regularization against collapse without negative sample pairs. Dual-Loop OS addresses this by integrating the Principle of Maximal Coding Rate Reduction ($\mathrm{MCR}^2$; \citealp{yu2020learning}) to enforce orthogonal subspace decomposition ($\mathcal{M}_{9\mathrm{D}} \perp \mathcal{S}_{4\mathrm{D}}$), providing formal discrete-time forward invariance directly bridged to a JEPA latent predictor.
